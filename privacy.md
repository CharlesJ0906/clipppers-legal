# Privacy Policy for Clippers

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Clippers is a personal tool for publishing short-form video clips to TikTok.

**Information collected.** When you connect a TikTok account, Clippers receives basic account information (such as your display name and account identifier) and access tokens that allow it to publish videos on your behalf.

**How it is used.** This information is used only to publish content you choose and to show which account is connected. It is not used for advertising.

**Storage.** Tokens and account information are stored locally on the operator's computer. They are not sold or shared with third parties.

**Retention and deletion.** Data is kept only while the account is connected. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings, and you can request deletion by contacting the address below.
